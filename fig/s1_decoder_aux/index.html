Giving the decoder something to measure between 160 m and 20 m

The soil-moisture decoder upsamples 14×14 tokens to 112×112 and has no measurement at any scale in between. It fills the gap with confident invention. S1 Decoder Aux feeds it Sentinel-1 backscatter, decomposed so that each decoder stage receives a real observation at its own grid.

The problem, in the order it was found

  1. The collapse is in the decoder, not the encoder

    Spatial variance survives attention almost intact, then dies on the way up. §16.1

  2. Because TerraMind is a ViT, every skip is the same size

    It patches once and never downsamples, so L3, L6, L9 and L12 are all 14×14. The decoder's "skip connections" are four views of one grid, interpolated up. Between 160 m and 20 m it holds no measurement at all. §33.2 · model.py:257-264, :530

  3. Something fills the vacuum, and it is not data

    Confident structure up to 0.19 m³/m³ — strongest at the flattest site, which is where real sub-tile variation should be weakest. §23

  4. Terrain was tried as the carrier, and failed

    HAND is reproducible (r ≥ 0.96 under 1 m of DEM noise) and irrelevant: r = −0.102 on level, and +0.088 on dynamics — wrong-signed, p = 0.0099, n = 877. TWI was disqualified earlier on stability. Stable ≠ useful. §32.9.4 · §32.10 · §32.11

  5. Daytime thermal was tried, and failed

    Landsat LST does not track soil moisture within a station (r = −0.077), because the daytime field is 0.967-coherent across seasons — a static landscape property wearing a temperature's clothes. §29.13 · §29.15

  6. But the signal being chased is real

    Stations ~400 m apart differ by a median 0.047 m³/m³, with the same sign on 95.8% of days.

    §32.10.2

  7. And the patchwise rebuild memorised its training stations

    pw_stage2a_L3 tracks shape but misses level — Pearson r² 0.45–0.64 while NSE is negative everywhere. The structural reading: the SM label is one pixel out of 112×112, so nothing teaches the decoder spatial pattern at all. §35.33.6

Two corrections to carry forward

The famous 0.0065 is obsolete. §16.1's decoder rel_spatial_std collapse to 0.0065 is still quoted in places, but §23 re-measured it at 0.09–0.52, with structure anti-correlated with the landscape. The decoder-side diagnosis survives; that particular number does not.

Memorisation may not be a supervision problem at all. §35.33.6 diagnoses it as station identity entering through the statics, and queues a statics ablation to test that. The ablation is eval-only and far cheaper than building an auxiliary arm. Run it first, or write down why not.

Why Sentinel-1 is the only candidate left

The requirement is narrow: something that varies in space at sub-tile scale and in time with the weather, and that exists everywhere. Almost nothing does both.

What the decoder actually receives

The decomposition, and why not raw σ⁰

Backscatter is a sum of three things the network cannot separate on its own — permanent texture, how wet the whole tile is, and how wet this spot is relative to its own norm. Feeding raw σ⁰ asks the decoder to learn that separation from one pixel of supervision. Feeding the decomposition hands it over.

sigma0(p,k)  =   c_k       +   (r_p - mu)   +   d(p,k)
                 permanent     how wet the      how wet THIS spot is
                 texture       whole tile is    versus its own norm

P        = 10^(sigma_dB/10)                       LINEAR power, never dB
T_l(p,k) = 10*log10( mean of P over the f x f block ),   f = 224/l
d(p,k)   = T_l(p,k) - r_p - c_k + mu

That is the double-centring projection D = (I − 11'/n) T (I − 11'/l²), computed per station × relative-orbit group × polarisation. It gives Σ_k d = 0 ∀p and Σ_p d = 0 ∀k, and an exact variance split Var(T) = Var(r) + Var(c) + Var(d) — verified numerically to 2×10⁻⁷ dB². Full derivation and worked examples in text/s1processing.md.

Seven channels per stage, not three

channelshapewhat it is
c_VVℓ×ℓpermanent texture, from the archive
d_VVℓ×ℓthis spot vs its own norm, most recent pass
d_CRℓ×ℓd_VH − d_VV exactly — free, by linearity of centring
w_VVscalarcatchment wetness state, broadcast
w_CRscalarcross-ratio wetness, broadcast
agescalardays since that pass
validscalar0 ⇒ the four above are 0; c_VV stays

w has to be fed back explicitly because double-centring deletes it from d by construction — and it is what lets a convolution express response × wetness rather than a flat offset. Broadcasting a scalar costs nothing, and a conv that sees d and age in the same tensor can discount stale d locally, which it cannot do from a global vector.

What was deliberately left out

droppedwhy
β, the d_VV − β·d_CR coefficient ≈ 993 × 4 numbers fitted offline — the same class of object the water-balance design was rejected for. Feed both terms and let one shared trained weight do the combining. β survives as the diagnostic that earned the design, not as code.
the up4 stage The most expensive conv in the decoder and the only stage with no measured input. Output is 112×112 @ 20 m, not 224×224 @ 10 m — 20 m is where S1 actually resolves. Claiming 10 m would be claiming the grid rather than the resolution.
c_VH as an input Still computed — d_VH needs it — but a static land-cover map, redundant with LULC and c_VV. Judgement call, cheap to revisit.

The safety property that makes this reviewable

Every new input slice is zero-initialised — conv.block[0].weight[:, -7:] = 0 — so at initialisation the forward pass is bit-identical to best.pt and §26's provenance gate applies unchanged. The same trick makes the ablation trivial: zero the slice at test time and you have removed exactly this stage and nothing else.

Note the width. §33.6 said [:, -3:]; the amendment widened it to seven. Getting that slice width wrong silently breaks the provenance gate rather than failing loudly.

The thermal head, and why S1 and LST never touch

S1 is input only. Thermal is target only. Nothing appears on both sides, so no leakage discipline is needed anywhere in the design.

head_sm    -> (B, n_depths, 112, 112) @ 20 m      the product
head_therm -> (B, 2, 112, 112)  day / night       auxiliary, deleted at inference
                avg_pool2d(7,7) -> 16x16 @ EXACTLY 140 m
                ECOSTRESS 70 m  -> 2x2 average   -> 140 m      exact on both sides

Two channels rather than one DTR channel, so unpaired passes still supervise. ISMN supervises the level, at one pixel, absolute; thermal supervises the pattern, dense and zero-sum. They do not compete for the same degree of freedom. The 7×7 pooling is exact — 112 = 7 × 16, no crop — which is what fixed the ragged 28→22 adaptive pooling in the original §33.7 spec.

Open risks, stated before building

Gate 1 is unrun, and it is still the only thing that can kill the design. Everything above is wiring for a hypothesis that has not been validated. Nothing should be built until it passes.

w_CR rests on an argument, not a number. The §33.5 "season%" column was misread as a vegetation fraction; it is a variance partition and sums to 100. So canopy contamination of w is plausible but unmeasured — unlike d, where 16–34% is measured directly against d_CR.

c_k temporal look-ahead is unresolved. The archive reference is built from all passes in the group, including ones after the prediction date.

Order of work is contested. The statics ablation tests the cheaper explanation for the same failure and has not been run.
