Details:

  1. Sentinel-2 L2A
     Script:   download_satellite_openeo.py  (exists, adapt for all stations)
     Bands:    B1 B2 B3 B4 B5 B6 B7 B8 B8A B9 B11 B12  (12 bands, B10 excluded)
     Patch:    224×224 pixels at 10 m = 2.24 km × 2.24 km centred on station
     Format:   one GeoTIFF per acquisition date per station
               {station_id}/S2L2A/{YYYYMMDD}.tif
     Notes:    cloud masks derived from these patches (CloudSEN12, precomputed on Snellius)
               TerraMind tokens precomputed from these patches (Snellius GPU)
               CDSE requires OAuth2 credentials (already configured)

  2. Sentinel-1 RTC
     Script:   download_satellite.py  (exists, adapt for all stations via CDSE)
     Channels: VH, VV  (2 channels, dB backscatter)
     Patch:    224×224 pixels at 10 m — same footprint as S2
     Format:   {station_id}/S1RTC/{YYYYMMDD}_{ASC|DESC}.tif
     Notes:    cloud-penetrating — no cloud masking needed
               ~12-day revisit (6-day with ascending + descending orbits combined)

  3. ERA5-Land daily
     Script:   download_era5land_gee.py  (exists)
     Variables (19):
       temperature 2m      — mean, min, max        (3 vars)
       dewpoint 2m         — mean, min, max        (3 vars)
       skin temperature    — mean, min, max        (3 vars)
       10 m U wind         — mean, min, max        (3 vars)
       10 m V wind         — mean, min, max        (3 vars)
       surface pressure    — mean                  (1 var)
       total precipitation — sum                   (1 var)
     Format:   {station_id}/era5_daily.nc  (time × 19 vars)
     Notes:    Yeo-Johnson Power Transform normalisation applied at training time
               (fit on training split only); params stored in normalization_stats.json
               GEE collection: ECMWF/ERA5_LAND/DAILY_AGGR

  4. SIF (TROPOMI)
     Script:   NEW — download_sif_tropomi.py  (needs writing)
     Variables: SIF radiance (mW/m²/sr/nm), SIF uncertainty
     Resolution: ~3.5 km — use value at station pixel; no spatial patch needed
     Format:   {station_id}/SIF/{YYYYMMDD}.nc
     Source:   ESA Copernicus Sentinel-5P TROPOMI product (SIF 740nm)
               OR pre-processed TROPOSIF dataset (Köhler et al.)
     Notes:    sparse — only cloud-free overpasses produce valid values
               ~46 tokens/year on average; gaps handled by sparse token injection
               missing days simply have no token in the temporal transformer sequence
               modality dropout p=0.5 during training

  5. TWSA (GRACE/GRACE-FO)
     Script:   NEW — download_twsa_grace.py  (needs writing)
     Variables: terrestrial water storage anomaly (cm), uncertainty
     Resolution: ~300 km — single value per station per month
     Format:   {station_id}/TWSA/{YYYY}.nc  (12 monthly values per year)
     Source:   NASA GES DISC — GRACE/GRACE-FO Level-3 mascon solutions
               JPL RL06.1 or GSFC mascons recommended
               URL: https://podaac.jpl.nasa.gov/dataset/TELLUS_GRAC-GRFO_MASCON_CRI_GRID_RL06.1_V3
     Notes:    ~12 tokens/year; mid-month DoY assignment for positional encoding
               (Jan=15, Feb=46, Mar=74, ...) as specified in architecture.md
               modality dropout p=0.5 during training

Details:

  6. DEM (Copernicus GLO-30)
     Script:   add to GEE batch download script
     Patch:    224×224 px at 10 m — same footprint as S2/S1
     Format:   {station_id}/DEM/dem.tif  (float32, elevation in metres)
     Notes:    static — download once per station
               GEE collection: COPERNICUS/DEM/GLO30

  7. LULC (ESA WorldCover)
     Script:   add to GEE batch download script
     Patch:    224×224 px at 10 m
     Format:   {station_id}/LULC/{YYYY}.tif  (uint8, class 0–9)
     Notes:    annual product; download one per year of station record
               GEE collection: ESA/WorldCover/v200  (2020, 2021 available)
               Use 2021 for all stations unless record predates 2020

  8. OpenLandMap-soildb
     Script:   NEW — download_soil_openlandmap.py  (needs writing)
     Patch:    74×74 pixels at 30 m = 2.22 km × 2.22 km centred on station
     Channels (7, stacked as multi-band GeoTIFF):
       0  clay              (wt%)
       1  sand              (wt%)
       2  silt              (wt%)
       3  SOC content       (g kg⁻¹)
       4  SOC density       (kg m⁻³)
       5  bulk density      (t m⁻³)
       6  pH (H₂O)
     Depth:    surface layer (0–30 cm)
     Format:   {station_id}/soil/soil_patch.tif  (float32, 7-band)
     Download: global GeoTIFFs from Zenodo (~2 GB compressed)
               clip 74×74 per station on Snellius — no per-station download needed
     Notes:    5-year temporal composites (2000, 2005, 2010, 2015, 2020, 2022)
               use composite closest to station mid-record year
               source paper: Hengl et al. (2026) ESSD
               DOI: https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.15470431

  Cloud masks:
    Model:    CloudSEN12 UNetMobV2_V2  (script: cloud_masking_inference.py, exists)
    Input:    S2 patch (224×224×12)
    Output:   {station_id}/CloudMask/{YYYYMMDD}.tif  (uint8, per-pixel cloud class)
    Used as:  per-token (16×16 px) key_padding_mask in TerraMind Latent Cross-Attention

  TerraMind tokens:
    Model:    TerraMind Base (frozen, 12 layers, 768-dim, 12 heads)
    Script:   precompute_terramind.py  (exists)
    Input:    S2 or S1 patch → 196 L12 tokens; cloud mask for S2
              DEM, LULC → 196 L12 tokens (no cloud mask)
    Output:   {station_id}/tokens/{YYYYMMDD}.pt  (shape: (196, 768), float16)
    Notes:    ~50 GB total replaces ~11 TB raw satellite for model training
              L3/L6/L9 intermediate features also saved for U-Net skip connections
