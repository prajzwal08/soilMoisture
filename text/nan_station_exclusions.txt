NaN Tier-1 Station Exclusions
==============================
Date: 2026-05-28
Analysis source: cleanup_tier1_failures.py (dry-run job 23164661)
Decision: exclude 17 stations entirely from training

Background
----------
After applying TerraMesh tier-1 filtering (discard tiles with > 50% invalid
pixels), 17 stations lose more than 50% of their S2L2A tiles. The surviving
tile counts are too low for meaningful temporal sequences at training time.
These stations are excluded entirely from the pipeline.

Root Cause
----------
All 17 stations sit at or near the boundary of an MGRS (Military Grid Reference
System) tile. The 224×224 pixel crop centred on the station straddles the edge
of the tile, so a large fraction of the crop falls outside the MGRS boundary
and is filled with DN=0 across all 12 bands. This is not a data quality issue —
the data for pixels inside the boundary is valid — but the tile geometry makes
the crop unusable for tokenization.

Criterion
---------
Excluded if: (S2L2A tiles deleted at tier-1) / (S2L2A tiles total) > 50 %

Pipeline impact after exclusion
--------------------------------
  Stations remaining : 1,048 − 17 = 1,031
  S2L2A tiles freed  : 8,038 tiles (all files in those station scratch dirs)
  sm_only stations   : 894 − 16 = 878
  sm_and_flux stations: 49 − 1  = 48

What will be deleted
--------------------
For each of the 17 stations above, the following will be removed:

  1. Entire scratch directory:
     /gpfs/scratch1/shared/pkhanal/satellite/{station}/
     (S2L2A, S1RTC, DEM, LULC, CloudMask subdirs)

  2. Entire data directory:
     /gpfs/work3/0/prjs1968/data/{split}/{station}/
     (ERA5Land, labels.nc, SIF, soil, TWSA subdirs)

Note on ICOS_DE-Tha (sm_and_flux)
----------------------------------
This is the only sm_and_flux station being excluded. It has flux tower data
(FLUXNET/ICOS) in addition to soil moisture. Its exclusion reduces sm_and_flux
from 49 to 48 stations but does not affect the sm_only or flux_only splits.

Note on ISMN_SNOTEL stations
-----------------------------
SNOTEL (Snow Telemetry) stations are high-elevation mountain sites in the US
Rocky Mountains and Alaska. They cluster around partial MGRS tile boundaries,
explaining why 9 of the 17 excluded stations are SNOTEL. Their soil moisture
measurements are also at high elevation with complex snow/ice dynamics —
excluding them simplifies training.
