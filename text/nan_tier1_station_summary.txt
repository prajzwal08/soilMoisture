Tier-1 Deletion Summary — Station-wise Breakdown
==================================================
Date: 2026-05-28
Source: text/tier1_deleted_records.csv  (25,362 records)

Key finding: S2L2A and S1RTC failures are largely on DIFFERENT stations.
  S2L2A failures are caused by MGRS tile boundary crops (fill pixels).
  S1RTC failures are caused by SAR swath edges (NaN pixels).
  They rarely co-occur on the same station at high rates.

  → 99.5% of S1RTC failures are on stations that are NOT being excluded.
