\begin{frame}{Conclusions\quad{\color{tumblue}Answers to the research questions}}
\small
\textbf{RQ1}\; {\color{tumblue}Do frozen GFM embeddings add skill beyond a multimodal driver baseline?}\\
Ans. \textbf{GFM embeddings carry a site-specific signal, comparable to climate.} Swapping satellite tokens between
stations raises ubRMSE by 37--38\,\% at every depth (ERA5 from another station: +15--30\,\%); reordering S1 in time
at the same station costs $\approx$0\,\%. Added skill still needs a drivers-only run.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{RQ2}\; {\color{tumblue}What does the model generalise to: places or processes?}\\
Ans. \textbf{New sites are slightly worse than seen stations.} Median per-station ubRMSE 0.048--0.050 at new sites
vs 0.043--0.047 at seen stations.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{RQ3}\; {\color{tumblue}How does skill vary across the landscape?}\\
Ans. \textbf{Timing is captured, level may not be.} At TxSON, r is 0.69--0.85 and ubRMSE 0.032--0.063 at all six
stations, while bias runs from $-$0.12 to +0.11 inside one 2.2\,km tile.

\vspace{6pt}
\textbf{RQ4}\; {\color{tumblue}Which modalities matter, at which depth?}\\
Ans. \textbf{Depth changes what the model relies on.} ERA5 dominates the surface; satellite matters equally at all
depths; soil, DEM and land cover gain weight at depth.
\end{frame}
